# Ethical Hacking Study Handbook

By Dr. Atul Tiwari
{: .byline }

Twenty chapters, one for each module of the CEH v13 syllabus. Read them in order, or jump to the one you are studying this week.

## How every chapter is built

- An opening anyone can follow: what the chapter is about, what it assumes, and why it matters.
- Tags on every section and key fact, so you know how to study it.
- "Try it" commands for the lab, "Remember" mnemonics, and a five-line recap per section.
- "Exam answer / In practice" boxes where the exam and the real world differ.
- "Updated" boxes, with the month and year, where the official courseware has fallen behind the standard, law or tool it describes. Each one also says what the exam still expects.
- A quiz and an automatically generated exam cheat sheet.
